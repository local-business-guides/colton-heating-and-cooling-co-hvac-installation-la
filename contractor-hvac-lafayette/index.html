Service details, timing, and common terms

The work depends on the condition of the equipment and what you need done. Here are useful points to discuss when you contact a contractor:

  • What it covers: Heating and cooling installation, repair, and maintenance for residential and commercial systems.
  • Typical timing: Emergency repair service is available 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. Repair and installation timing depends on the issue, the work involved, and scheduling. Ask about the expected next steps when you call.
  • What affects cost: The type and size of equipment, the part or repair needed, the complexity of installation, and access to the system can all affect the overall scope and cost. Ask for a clear explanation of any proposed work.
  • How to compare providers: Check that the company handles your service need, ask how it will diagnose the problem, and compare the proposed work and timing.

A few terms may come up during a service conversation. HVAC means heating, ventilation, and air conditioning. Installation is setting up a new system or replacing existing equipment. Maintenance is planned service intended to check and care for a system. Emergency repair is service for an urgent equipment problem, including calls outside standard business hours.
