What a service visit can include

The work depends on whether you need a new system, a repair, or ongoing upkeep. A contractor may inspect the equipment, identify the concern, explain options, and carry out the agreed work. Installation planning and repairs can involve different tasks, so ask what is included before work begins.

Key facts to keep in mind:
- What it covers: Commercial HVAC installation, repair, and maintenance.
- Typical timing: Timing depends on the request. An urgent breakdown is different from a planned tune-up or installation; the issue, equipment, access, and parts needed can all affect how long work takes.
- What affects cost: The type and size of the system, the scope of the work, the parts required, and whether the request is for repair, maintenance, or replacement. Ask for an explanation of the proposed work and its cost before authorizing it.
- How to compare providers: Ask about experience with the kind of equipment at your property, what the inspection covers, what options are available, and how the provider will explain findings and next steps.

You may hear a few common terms. HVAC means heating, ventilation, and air conditioning. Maintenance is planned service intended to check and care for equipment. Installation means setting up a new system or replacement equipment. Ask the contractor to explain any other term that isn't clear.
