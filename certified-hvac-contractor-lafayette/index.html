What to expect from HVAC service

The work depends on the issue. A visit may involve checking the equipment, identifying a repair need, performing maintenance, or discussing installation if replacement is being considered. The technician should explain the findings and proposed work before you decide how to proceed.

Key facts for your decision:
- Service coverage: HVAC repair, heating service, air conditioning service, maintenance, and installation are the main areas to discuss.
- Typical timing: Appointment timing depends on the problem and schedule. Emergency repairs are available 24/7, but ask what response and work timing to expect for your situation.
- Cost factors: The type of equipment, the fault or maintenance needed, parts, and the amount of labor can affect cost. Request the scope and cost before authorizing work.
- Comparing providers: Ask what inspection or work is included, how the technician will explain findings, and whether the recommendation addresses the specific symptoms you reported. Compare the proposed work, not only the initial description of the issue.
